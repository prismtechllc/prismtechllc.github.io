Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

PlayPlan is made by Prism Technology Consulting, LLC, Michigan, USA ("we"). PlayPlan helps parents who know each other arrange play dates. We collect only what the app needs, never sell it, and show no ads.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressSign in with a one-time code
Family name, and the names, interests and notes of family members you addShow your family to families you connect with
Play dates, answers and chat messagesArrange play dates
Push notification tokenSend you invitations and messages
Place searches (only when you turn on place suggestions) and your approximate locationSuggest nearby places; your location is not stored
Feedback emails you sendAnswer you and improve the app

We don't collect contacts, photos, ages, birthdays or advertising IDs, and we don't track you across apps.

Children

PlayPlan is for parents and guardians only. Children don't use the app or have accounts. Parents choose what to enter about their children (first name, interests, notes such as allergies). Enter only what other families need to know.

Who can see your information

  • Your family's profile: only families you have connected with by invitation.
  • A play date: the host family and the families invited to it. Guests who aren't connected see names only.
  • Disconnecting from a family stops them seeing your profile.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), hosted in the United States (Ohio)
  • Expo (push notifications and app updates)
  • Google Gmail (sending sign-in codes and receiving feedback)
  • Photon by Komoot, based on OpenStreetMap (place suggestions)
  • Apple and Google (app stores and notification delivery)

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Play dates, with their answers and chat: deleted 30 days after they end or are cancelled.
  • Invite codes: deleted 30 days after they are used or expire.
  • Your account: kept until you delete it in the app (Us, Delete account). Deleting removes your personal data. If you're the last parent in your family, the family's profile and kids are removed too, except first names still shown on past play dates, which are deleted with those play dates within 30 days.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can see, correct and delete your information in the app, and email us for a copy of it or with any request. We answer within 30 days.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, we process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract) and, for notifications and place suggestions, with your consent, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings. Your data is stored in the United States. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

If you're in California, we don't sell or share personal information, and you have the right to know, delete and correct it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules let each family see only what's shared with it. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

We'll post changes here and update the date above; significant changes will be shown in the app. Questions: playplan.mobile.app@gmail.com.
