Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

PlayPlan is made by Prism Technology Consulting, LLC, Michigan, USA ("we"). PlayPlan helps parents who know each other arrange playdates. We collect only what the app needs, never sell it, and show no ads.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address (and, if you sign in with Google or Facebook, the email they share with us)Sign in
Family name, and the names, interests and notes of family members you addShow your family to families you connect with
Playdates, answers, chat messages and cancellation reasonsArrange playdates
Who shares your account (their sign-in emails)Let several people, like both parents, use one family account
Push notification token and your playdate alert settingSend you invitations, messages and the alerts you choose
Place searches (only when you turn on place suggestions) and your approximate locationSuggest nearby places; your location is not stored
Feedback you send in the app, with the reply email you give, your account email and the app versionAnswer you and improve the app. It is emailed to us and not stored in the app's database

We don't collect contacts, photos, ages, birthdays or advertising IDs, and we don't track you across apps.

Children

PlayPlan is for parents and guardians only. Children don't use the app or have accounts. Parents choose what to enter about their children (first name, interests, notes such as allergies). Enter only what other families need to know.

Who can see your information

  • Your family's profile: only families you have connected with by invitation.
  • A playdate: the host family and the families invited to it. Guests who aren't connected see names only.
  • People you share your account with see everything in your family, as you do. The primary account holder also sees their emails.
  • Disconnecting from a family stops them seeing your profile.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), hosted in the United States (Ohio)
  • Expo (push notifications and app updates)
  • Google Gmail (sending sign-in codes and feedback emails)
  • Google and Facebook (sign-in, only if you choose them)
  • Photon by Komoot, based on OpenStreetMap (place suggestions)
  • Apple and Google (app stores and notification delivery)

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Playdates, with their answers and chat: deleted 14 days after they end or are cancelled.
  • Invite codes: deleted 30 days after they are used or expire.
  • Partner invite codes: deleted 30 days after they are used or expire.
  • Feedback: kept in our email inbox only as long as needed to handle it.
  • Your account: kept until you delete it in the app (Help, Account, Delete account). Deleting removes your personal data. As the primary account holder, deleting also removes the family's profile and children and ends access for everyone you shared it with, except first names still shown on past playdates, which are deleted with those playdates within 14 days. If you joined someone's account, you can leave it in the app (Help, Account, Leave account); the family stays with the others.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules let each family see only what's shared with it. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

We'll post changes here and update the date above; significant changes will be shown in the app. Questions: use Send feedback in the app, under Help.
